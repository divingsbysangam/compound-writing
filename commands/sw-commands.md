# Divings by Sangam Workflows

## Orchestration
- `sw-scribe` — Route an open-ended request to the smallest useful workflow.
- `sw-emergent` — Compose a custom workflow when no single tool fits.

## Writing Home And Context
- `sw-setup-project` — Initialize a piece folder with `VOICE.md`, `STYLE.md`, and `drafts/`.
- `sw-onboarding` — Calibrate or refresh voice and editorial standards.
- `sw-save` — Capture a confirmed preference or editorial lesson into `VOICE.md` or `STYLE.md`.

## Ideation And Sourcing
- `sw-brainstorm` — Surface raw ideas in tech/Salesforce or reflective mode.
- `sw-interview` — Interrogate Sangam for real build memories, numbers, and physical scenes.
- `sw-thesis` — Formulate one arguable, falsifiable claim.
- `sw-promise` — Clarify reader takeaway and test the "So What?" chain.
- `sw-outline` — Structure material into 4-part short-form or 6-beat long-form arcs.
- `sw-hook` — Create scene, memory, confession, or specific result openers.
- `sw-transition` — Build causal bridges between sections.
- `sw-analogy` — Generate concrete physical analogies for dry architecture.
- `sw-simplify` — Strip corporate abstraction into plain English.

## Drafting And Revising
- `sw-draft` — Build a structural scaffold (beats, tension, facts) without finished prose.
- `sw-bluf` — Check the First Paragraph Rule (core tension in first 2–3 sentences).
- `sw-dev-edit` — Check structure, stakes, symptom-before-diagnosis, and self-implication.
- `sw-line-edit` — Polish syntax, sentence length variance, and connective flow.
- `sw-voice-check` — Audit draft against `VOICE.md` (mandatory contractions, five human signals).
- `sw-ai-check` — Enforce zero em dashes, eliminate banned words, and remove synthetic tells.
- `sw-tracks` — Remove scaffolding notes, bracketed assumptions, and process residue.
- `sw-final-pass` — Execute the 15-point pre-delivery verification checklist.

## Pressure-Testing And Lenses
- `sw-objections` — Surface skeptical developer/architect pushback.
- `sw-asshole` — Attack weak or under-supported claims uncharitably.
- `sw-hemingway` — Cut filler words and enforce maximum verbal economy.
- `sw-hitchcock` — Test pacing and tension across the narrative.
- `sw-reader` — Audit the first-time reader journey for friction or unexplained jumps.
- `sw-mom` — Test clarity for non-technical readers.
- `sw-sedaris` — Identify places for self-implication, wry parentheticals, or humor.
- `sw-sorkin` — Review dialogue rhythm, banter, and momentum.
- `sw-vonnegut` — Apply core story fundamentals.
- `sw-panel` — Synthesize multiple reviewer perspectives on a single draft.
- `sw-debate` — Pit reviewers against each other across multi-round challenges.

## Platform Extensions
- `sw-atomize` — Transform approved Substack essay into a Koe/Shipper LinkedIn caption + Gamma carousel.
- `sw-sf-check` — Verify technical and release claims against the official Salesforce Release Notes PDF.
