---\nname: sw-emergent\ndescription: Compose a custom writing or revision workflow when no standard tool fits.\n---\n\n# Emergent

Composes an ad-hoc pipeline across multiple skills for unique editorial needs.

## Process
1. Assess the goal, current state of material, and desired outcome.
2. Select 2 to 4 skills in sequence (e.g. `sw-interview` -> `sw-thesis` -> `sw-outline`).
3. Enforce Sangam context (`VOICE.md` and `STYLE.md`) across all intermediate stages.\n
