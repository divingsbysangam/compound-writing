---\nname: sw-bluf\ndescription: Audit whether the core tension appears within the first 2-3 sentences.\n---\n\n# BLUF (First Paragraph Rule)

Evaluates whether the piece buries its lead.

## Audit
1. Read the opening paragraph.
2. Confirm whether the central tension or paradox lands within the first 2 to 3 sentences.
3. If delayed, reorder the opening to put the mechanism or conflict immediately in front of the reader.\n
