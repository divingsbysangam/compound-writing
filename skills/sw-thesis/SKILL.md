---\nname: sw-thesis\ndescription: Formulate one central, arguable, falsifiable claim.\n---\n\n# Thesis

Defines the single core claim of the piece.

## Invariants
- **One claim, not one topic**: Must fit in a single sentence.
- **Falsifiable**: A knowledgeable peer or senior architect could plausibly say "No, I disagree."
- If multiple claims compete, select the strongest and save the other for the next issue.\n
